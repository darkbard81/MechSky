Shared production prompt
------------------------

Create exactly one square 2x2 sprite grid with four rooted idle frames of the
same sleek humanoid mech. Preserve the canonical white ceramic armor, deep navy
panels, graphite joints, narrow cyan visor or rear power light, twin swept head
fins, broad angular shoulders, long athletic legs, small amber accents, and the
clean high-detail anime-mecha rendering. Do not redesign, simplify, or make
pixel art.

Reading order is neutral, slight mechanical breathing compression, neutral
return with a tiny attached cyan-light intensity change, and subtle release
with attached antenna/armor secondary motion. Both feet and the pelvis stay
rooted. No step, foot lift, attack, dash, recoil, lateral travel, detached FX,
particles, or motion symbols.

Use exactly four equal invisible cells. Keep one complete full-body mech
centered in each cell at one camera distance and standing-equivalent scale.
Occupy only the central 60-64 percent of cell height and at most 58 percent of
cell width, keep the feet/contact line near 82 percent of cell height, and leave
broad empty margins. Nothing may touch or cross a cell boundary.

The complete canvas background is uniform flat solid #FF00FF. No gradient,
texture, noise, floor, shadow, reflection, grid, separator, box, text, label,
UI, watermark, signature, extra image, weapon, prop, scenery, or magenta detail
on the mech.

Direction-specific prompts
--------------------------

down: Face toward the viewer with front chest armor and cyan visor visible,
using an orthographic-like 3/4 camera from slightly above.

left: Use a true left-facing side silhouette. Head, visor wedge, chest, knees,
and toes point left. Match the accepted down sheet's scale, feet line, lighting,
and animation phases.

right: Use a true right-facing side silhouette. Head, visor wedge, chest,
knees, and toes point right. Match the accepted down and left sheets' scale,
feet line, lighting, and animation phases.

up: Face directly away. Show the rear head shell, back armor, cyan rear power
light, shoulder backs, spine/waist mechanisms, rear legs, and heels. No face,
front visor, or front chest plate may be visible. Match the accepted down
sheet's scale, feet line, lighting, and animation phases.
